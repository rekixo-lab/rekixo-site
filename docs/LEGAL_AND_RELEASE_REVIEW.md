# REKIXO legal/privacy and trust review checklist

**Working checklist only — not legal advice or a substitute for counsel.** No legal status, tax number, business address verification or privacy compliance certification is inferred from source files.

## Brand and identity
- [ ] Confirm legal supplier name / business form and whether it may be published.
- [ ] Confirm whether any address should be public or restricted to invoices and agreements.
- [ ] Verify official mailboxes receive and reply to support/privacy notices.
- [ ] Ensure no invented team size, certifications, client testimonials or projects.

## Company site: free enquiry and paid history
- [ ] Verify the normal enquiry is clearly free, with no payment prerequisite.
- [ ] New paid priority booking is not advertised until service-level terms, response SLA, price and refund rule are confirmed.
- [ ] Preserve recovery/support for prior paid transactions, and verify no duplicate charging.
- [ ] Review D1/Gmail/Cloudflare/analytics actual data flow against public Privacy Notice.
- [ ] Adopt a documented retention schedule for enquiries, payment records, support and audit data; do not fabricate fixed periods.
- [ ] Confirm project IP ownership and licensing exclude pre-existing Rekixo technology unless explicitly agreed.
- [ ] Legal counsel reviews jurisdiction, complaints, customer support, cancellation and consumer protection wording before release.

## Consumer applications
- [ ] FinWorkar permits account deletion without mandatory feedback; verifies data purging/retention and both app and web request paths.
- [ ] FinWorkar backend deletion change is deployed and acceptance-tested *before* the Android/website experience is described as current.
- [ ] LudoPaws verifies child-directed ad SDK, age handling, Play purchases, refund/revoke reconciliation, consent and account deletion before production availability claims.
- [ ] Product store declarations, screenshots and privacy notices match *the actual published build*.

## Developer platforms
- [ ] AR3D customer contractual controller/processor duties, tenant ownership, R2/D1 retention and handover.
- [ ] GeoLive customer location collection, legal basis/consent, precise-location minimization, short-lived keys, deletion/retention and cross-runtime boundaries.
- [ ] EntroNex outcome/proof retention, third-party audit and limitations before public security-critical usage.

## Public links and SEO
- [ ] Sitemap includes only intended canonical/indexable pages.
- [ ] Referral hosting pages disclose sponsorship and do not imply Rekixo requires a particular provider.
- [ ] Public link and privacy policy checks pass on 320–1920px and both display modes.
- [ ] Obtain confirmation of deployed commit SHA from live Worker assets after merge.
