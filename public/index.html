FinWorkar

One app for finding work and finding nearby workers.

FinWorkar is built around local work discovery. Workers can create service posts and improve visibility. Employers or customers can inspect nearby posts and connect through the app.

Open FinWorkar See How It Works
Nearby discoveryPublic services can be browsed, while location permission improves nearby distance and ranking.
Service postingWorkers add their work, price, work area and images, then share the post to reach more people.
Private app callsPeople can connect through direct in-app calling without requiring a public personal mobile number.
Work profile & verificationEducation, email, work profile and profile photo steps support a verified-badge flow.
Alerts, stories & growthSaved nearby alerts, stories, views, likes and post guidance help workers stay active and visible.
First-post & premium pathsA first-post path helps users get started; optional post plans add credits, pinned placement and premium visibility.
