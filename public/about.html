Why Rekixo Exists

Rekixo designs and builds independent software products around real problems: finding local work, managing real-estate projects, live location services, multiplayer games and verifiable digital outcomes.

It is an independently developed, founder-led brand. We do not present ourselves as a large agency, claim an unverified team size, or imply that every product is generally available today.

Five Product Families

  • FinWorkar — local work and service discovery.
  • Rekixo AR3D — project sites, inventory, plotting, builder controls and optional 3D experiences.
  • GeoLive — location infrastructure for developer integrations.
  • LudoPaws — Android Ludo game; store availability is subject to release approval.
  • EntroNex — research-stage cryptographic outcome infrastructure, not independently audited.

Each product has a separate codebase, audience and release process. See the portfolio for details and availability caveats.
