Builder Admin

Give the builder control without exposing the factory.

Builder Admin is intentionally scoped. It gives a project team the operational controls they need while mapper, CAD, platform routing and other Rekixo-only controls stay protected.

Builder can manage

Day-to-day project operations that should stay current for customers.
