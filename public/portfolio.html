Product 01

FinWorkar

FinWorkar is a local jobs, workers and services marketplace. People can browse nearby services, workers can create service posts, and users can connect through direct in-app calls without making a personal mobile number public.

The product also includes work profiles, a verification flow, saved nearby alerts, stories, post views and likes, Call Time rewards, referral flows, first-post support, optional post plans and premium visibility. The Android app is connected to backend services and operational admin controls.

Nearby DiscoveryLocation-aware service posts, categories, search and saved nearby alerts.
Service PostingWork area, price, images, sharing and growth guidance for workers.
Private App CallsDirect in-app calling without requiring the worker's personal number to be public.
Worker GrowthVerification, stories, Call Time rewards, referral and premium visibility paths.

Product 02

Rekixo AR3D

Rekixo AR3D is a reusable multi-project real-estate platform that turns masterplans into interactive customer websites with searchable plots, live Available / Booked / Sold status, plot details, gallery, location and customer actions.

Behind the customer site, Rekixo Super Admin manages projects, access, profiles, domains, Plot Mapper, dimensions, Geo Mapper, publishing, share previews and recovery assets. Builder Admin remains scoped for daily project operations. Optional 3D experiences are handled by the separate Rekixo AR3D Engine.

Customer Project SitesBranded project layouts with searchable plot inventory and customer-facing details.
Builder AdminProject-scoped controls for live status, gallery, contact details and enabled workflows.
Super AdminOne control plane for provisioning, mapping, configuration, publishing and recovery.
Geo & 3D ReadySatellite calibration, geographic layers and optional isolated 3D Engine integration.
