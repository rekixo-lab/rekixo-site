Privacy Policy

How Rekixo handles client and project information.

Rekixo builds Android apps, web apps, dashboards, and custom digital systems. This policy explains what information we collect, why we use it, and how clients can contact us about their data.

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Information We Collect

When you contact Rekixo or submit a project request, we may collect your name, mobile number, email address, business name, project description, and any files or details you choose to share.

During a project, we may also receive product requirements, workflow details, brand assets, app content, access credentials shared by you, and technical information required to design, build, test, or support the software.

How We Use Information

  • To understand your software, app, website, dashboard, or custom system requirement.
  • To reply with project scope, estimate direction, delivery plan, or support guidance.
  • To design, develop, test, deliver, maintain, and improve agreed work.
  • To keep records of communication, invoices, approvals, milestones, and handover details.
  • To protect Rekixo, clients, users, and systems from misuse, fraud, or security issues.

Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell client information. We may share limited information with trusted tools or providers only when needed for contact forms, hosting, analytics, domain/hosting setup, app distribution, payment records, or project delivery.

Examples may include form services, email providers, cloud hosting, domain providers, payment processors, Play Store or app distribution services, and collaboration tools. These providers process data under their own policies.

How Website Enquiries Are Handled

When you submit a project request through rekixo.com, the information you enter is processed by Rekixo's Cloudflare Worker and recorded in a Cloudflare D1 database. Rekixo uses Google Workspace's Gmail API to send a notification to its business inbox. A request can be saved even when email notification delivery fails.

For previously completed advance-booking checkouts, Razorpay handled the payment interface. New paid priority booking is currently paused on the public enquiry page. Rekixo's website records relevant booking, order and payment identifiers, statuses and processing events for request handling, verification, support and records. Payment card details are entered into the payment provider's checkout, not the Rekixo enquiry form.

Different Rekixo products, including FinWorkar and LudoPaws, have their own data practices and product-specific privacy notices. This document describes the Rekixo company website and related project enquiries; it does not replace those separate product policies.

Website Preferences, Analytics and Embedded Content

The website stores your selected light or dark display preference in browser local storage. Depending on the pages you visit, the site may load Cloudflare Web Analytics or embedded map content; those services can receive technical request information under their own privacy terms.

We do not use your project enquiry to sell advertising profiles. If the website introduces new non-essential tracking or marketing cookies, Rekixo will review the appropriate notice and preference controls before enabling them. You can also manage browser storage through your browser settings.

Security and Retention

We use practical safeguards such as limited access, account protection, and careful handling of shared credentials. No online method is perfectly secure, so clients should avoid sharing unnecessary sensitive information unless it is required for the project.

Website enquiries, active project records and payment or accounting records may require different retention periods. Rekixo keeps information only as reasonably needed for the relevant enquiry, agreed work, support, security or applicable record-keeping obligations. You can request review or deletion of information that no longer needs to be retained.

Your Choices

You can ask us to access, update, correct or delete your project enquiry information, subject to reasonable identity verification and any applicable legal or record-keeping obligations. If your project uses end-user data, Rekixo can help design privacy-aware software, but the client remains responsible for their own collection and use of that information.

Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, write to ai@rekixo.com or use the contact form.
